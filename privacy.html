Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Orlyo is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Who we are

Orlyo Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17240631). We operate the Orlyo mobile application and website at orlyo.app. For the purposes of UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Orlyo Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through our services.

2. What data we collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, password (encrypted), profile photo, job title, industry, skills, experience and career goals.
  • Location data: City and country (used to match you with mentors in compatible timezones). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Career data: Goals, milestones, session notes, coaching conversations, gap analysis results and career signals.
  • AI memory: To personalise your experience, Orla derives and stores durable insights from your coaching conversations (for example your career stage, working style and progress). You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of this data — see sections 7 and 8.
  • Communication data: Messages exchanged with mentors or mentees through the Orlyo platform.
  • Payment data: Orlyo Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase; we do not receive or store your card details. Mentor session payments (where available) are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store card numbers.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, features used and general analytics.
  • Device data: Device type, operating system version and app version.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide and improve the Orlyo service.
  • Match you with appropriate mentors or mentees using our AI system.
  • Power Orla, our AI career companion, to provide personalised guidance that remembers your context between sessions.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Send important service notifications.
  • Analyse career signals and generate market intelligence relevant to your goals.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. AI features and data processing

Orlyo uses artificial intelligence powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use AI features, relevant profile and conversation data is sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic processes this data as a data processor on our behalf and does not use your data to train their models. Some voice features use ElevenLabs to generate audio, and some job and market data is retrieved via third-party search providers.

5. Data sharing

We share your data only with: your connected mentor or mentee (as necessary to provide the service); trusted service providers including Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat, Apple, Stripe, ElevenLabs, Daily.co and Expo; and where required by law. We will never sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

We retain your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days. Certain financial and transaction records are retained for up to 7 years as required by UK financial and tax law; where we retain these, we anonymise them so they are no longer linked to your identity.

7. Your rights (UK and EU)

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your personal data. You can delete your account and associated personal data directly within the app at any time. To exercise any other right, contact privacy@orlyo.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. US residents (California and other states)

If you are a resident of the United States, additional rights may apply under state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request access to and deletion of your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can delete your account and data within the app, or contact privacy@orlyo.app to exercise these rights.

9. Children's privacy

Orlyo is not intended for persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact us immediately if you believe we have done so.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will notify you of significant changes by email or through the app.

11. Complaints

If you have concerns, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
